---
title: "Domain Text Literals: A Readable Boundary for Embedded Languages"
description: The design background for domain text literals and their relationship to TPL and specific domain friendliness.
---

Domain text literals let an identifier introduce a raw text body that a domain processor can interpret. This makes embedded formats readable while keeping the boundary explicit in XGo source.

~~~xgo
json`{"name": "Ken", age: 15}`
~~~

The design grew from the need to support grammar-oriented text processing and formats that should remain visually close to their native notation. [Domain text literals](/docs/reference/domain-text-literals) documents the syntax and semantics.

TPL provides the grammar-based processing model behind several such formats. Its [data and text examples](/docs/examples/data-and-text) show domain text, DQL, and template programs.
